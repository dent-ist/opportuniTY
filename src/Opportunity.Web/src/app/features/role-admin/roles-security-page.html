Who may see restricted documents

A document coded as privileged, confidential or another restriction is visible only to the roles ticked for it, and only when every restriction on it allows one of their roles. Ethical walls hide documents from their members whatever the role. Tick a box to change it; it saves at once. Arrow keys move between boxes and Space ticks or clears one.
