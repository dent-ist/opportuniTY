# Metrics, dashboards and alerts

The consistency and pipeline signals of baseline §7, §21, §26 and §28 (E19-T05, #161; ADR-017 §5–§6). Outbox lag,
index lag and the search watermark drive the service level objectives; everything below is collected by the
`observability` Compose profile ([deploy/docker-compose/README.md](../../deploy/docker-compose/README.md#observability-profile))
or by an operator's own OTLP/Prometheus stack.

- **Application metrics** are defined once in `OpportunityMetricCatalog`
  (`src/Opportunity.Application/Telemetry`) and exported over OTLP. Prometheus names: dots become underscores, the
  unit is appended (`s` → `_seconds`), counters end in `_total` and histograms have `_bucket`/`_count`/`_sum`; attribute
  keys become labels the same way (`opportunity.lane` → `opportunity_lane`).
- **Infrastructure metrics** come from `postgres_exporter`, `elasticsearch_exporter` (reads OpenSearch) and the RabbitMQ
  `rabbitmq_prometheus` plugin.
- **Dashboards** are provisioned from the repository: `deploy/docker-compose/observability/grafana/dashboards`,
  generated by `generate-dashboard.py` (edit the script, not the JSON; a unit test fails when they differ).
  *opportuniTY overview* is the home dashboard; *opportuniTY search consistency and pipeline* holds the signals below.
- **Alerts** are Prometheus rules in `deploy/docker-compose/observability/alerts.yaml`. Every alert has a runbook
  ([alerts.md](alerts.md)) and an induced-failure case in `alerts.test.yaml` that makes it fire (and a healthy case that
  must not): `promtool test rules alerts.test.yaml`, run by `ObservabilityRuleTests` and CI. Routing (Alertmanager,
  e-mail, paging) is the operator's choice and is not part of the Compose profile.

## Cardinality

Labels are enums, lanes, statuses, queue names, worker types and job types. `opportunity_workspace_id` appears only on
the per-workspace generation and lag gauges and is bounded per process (`Telemetry:MaxWorkspaceAttributeValues`,
default 20, the rest report as `other`). Document, job, user, message and correlation IDs are never labels; they are on
spans and logs. Backlog gauges are summed over all workspaces.

transaction at a time; with several dispatchers each reports the same totals, so queries take `max` over instances.

## Service level objectives and alerts

Targets are baseline §17/§26 targets; absolute latency on developer hardware is comparative only (Q-44, Q-70).
*Critical* alerts page; *warning* alerts open a ticket.

| Alert | Fires when | Severity | Runbook |
|---|---|---|---|
| `InteractiveCommitToSearchableSlow` | interactive commit → searchable p95 > 1 s for 10 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#interactivecommittosearchableslow) |
| `SecurityProjectionLagHigh` | security projection lag p95 > 5 s for 5 min (Q-10) | critical | [alerts.md](alerts.md#securityprojectionlaghigh) |
| `BulkIndexLagHigh` | a workspace's oldest unsearchable change is > 2 min old for 10 min (§26) | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#bulkindexlaghigh) |
| `SearchWatermarkStuck` | generations pending and the watermark has not moved for 10 min (§28) | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#searchwatermarkstuck) |
| `OutboxOldestAgeHigh` | oldest undispatched outbox row > 60 s for 1 min | warning | [re-dispatch-stuck-work.md](re-dispatch-stuck-work.md#outboxoldestagehigh) |
| `DeadLetterQueueNotEmpty` | any `*.dlq` or `*.parking` queue depth > 0 | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#deadletterqueuenotempty) |
| `IndexQueueWithoutConsumers` | an `index.*` lane queue has no consumer for 5 min | critical | [alerts.md](alerts.md#indexqueuewithoutconsumers) |
| `DeadLetterRecorderDown` | `opportunity.dead-letter.record` has no consumer for 5 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#deadletterrecorderdown) |
| `IndexChunkTasksFailed` | Failed IndexChunkTasks for 5 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#indexchunktasksfailed) |
| `IndexChunkTaskRunningTooLong` | an IndexChunkTask Running ≥ 15 min (ADR-010 §7.6) | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#indexchunktaskrunningtoolong) |
| `JobChunkRunningTooLong` | a job chunk Running ≥ 15 min (ADR-010 §7.6) | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#jobchunkrunningtoolong) |
| `JobChunksFailed` | a chunk failed permanently in the last 15 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#jobchunksfailed) |
| `WorkerHeartbeatStale` | a worker type's heartbeat > 2 min old for 2 min | critical | [alerts.md](alerts.md#workerheartbeatstale) |
| `SearchLatencyHigh` | simple search p95 > 1 s or complex p95 > 3 s for 15 min (§17) | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#searchlatencyhigh) |
| `ApiErrorBudgetBurn` | API 5xx ratio > 14.4 × 0.1 % over 1 h and 5 min (§17 99.9 %) | critical | [alerts.md](alerts.md#apierrorbudgetburn) |
| `WalArchiveLagHigh` | last WAL archive > 4 min ago while rows change, for 1 min (RPO 5 min) | critical | [alerts.md](alerts.md#walarchivelaghigh) |
| `WalArchiveFailing` | `archive_command` failures for 5 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#walarchivefailing) |
| `PostgresReplicationLagHigh` | a standby > 60 s behind for 5 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#postgresreplicationlaghigh) |
| `OpenSearchBulkRejections` | write thread-pool rejections in the last 5 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#opensearchbulkrejections) |
| `OpenSearchHeapHigh` | heap > 90 % for 15 min | warning | [alerts.md](alerts.md#opensearchheaphigh) |

Notes on the definitions:

- *Interactive lag* uses the commit → searchable histogram (observations of completed work); *bulk lag* uses the index
  lag gauge, which keeps growing when nothing completes, so a stopped pipeline alerts too.
- *WAL archive lag*: PostgreSQL exposes the time of the last successful archive, not the age of the oldest unarchived
  segment. With `archive_timeout ≤ 60 s` (E19-T08) a segment is archived within a minute of being written, so "last
  archive older than 4 minutes while rows are changing" means WAL is waiting. An idle database writes no WAL and does
  not alert; a database without archiving reports no archive age and nothing alerts.
- *DLQ > 0*: the broker keeps dead-lettered messages 14 days for diagnostics. The alert clears when the queue is purged
  after the PostgreSQL rows behind it are settled ([replay-failed-work.md](replay-failed-work.md#dead-letter-queues)).
